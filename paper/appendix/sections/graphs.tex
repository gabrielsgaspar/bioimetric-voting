\section{Additional Graphs and Plots}
\label{app:additional-graphs-plots}

This appendix collects descriptive figures that complement the main-text survey discussion. Figures~\ref{fig:appendix-trust-pca-variance} and \ref{fig:appendix-democracy-pca-variance} report the share of explained variance by principal component for the institutional-trust and democracy survey blocks. The trust block is visibly more one-dimensional: its first component dominates the remaining components, while the democracy block leaves more variance for the second component. That pattern is one reason the democracy results in the main text are interpreted more cautiously.
