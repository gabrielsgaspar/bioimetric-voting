\section{Alternative Estimator Comparisons}
\label{app:alternative-estimators}

The main text uses the BJS imputation event study as the baseline staggered-adoption estimator \citep{Borusyak2024RevisitiEventStudy}. This appendix compares the baseline imputation paths to Callaway-Sant'Anna, dynamic TWFE, and de Chaisemartin-D'Haultfoeuille alternatives \citep{Callaway2020DifferenDifferenWith,deChaisemartin2020TwoWayFixed}. Across outcomes, the broad pattern is stable: BVR produces an immediate contraction in the registry and a persistent shift away from lower-education registrants and toward higher-education registrants. The main difference across estimators is precision at the longest horizons, where support becomes thin.
